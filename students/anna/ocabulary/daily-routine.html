Daily Routine 🧠

Useful words and phrases from our lessons.

Daily activities

wake up

Meaning: to stop sleeping

Example: I usually wake up at 7 o'clock.

get ready

Meaning: to prepare yourself for something

Example: I get ready for work after breakfast.

go to work

Meaning: to travel to your workplace

Example: I go to work at 8:30.

Useful chunks 💬

  • have breakfast — I have breakfast at 8.
  • get home — I usually get home at 6.
  • go to bed — I go to bed at 11.
