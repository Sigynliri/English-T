1. Responsibilities & Business Impact

🧠 Vocabulary

  • business impact — влияние на бизнес
  • impact the business — влиять на бизнес
  • the business scales — бизнес масштабируется

❓ Questions

  1. What are your key responsibilities?
  2. How does your work impact the business?
  3. How do you measure your impact?
  4. What results are you responsible for?

💬 Useful frames

I’m responsible for...

My work has a direct impact on...

I like to see how my work impacts the business...

I want to understand how my work fits into the bigger picture...

2. Candidate Profiles

🧠 Vocabulary

  • candidate profiles — профили кандидатов
  • evaluation criteria — критерии оценки

❓ Questions

  1. What information should a recruiter include in candidate profiles?
  2. How do you usually create a strong candidate profile?
  3. What evaluation criteria would you use for a senior candidate?
  4. Should the evaluation criteria be the same for every candidate? Why / why not?

When evaluating candidates, I usually focus on...

The key criteria would be...
