4. Interview Question Patterns

These questions help explore experience, decision-making and work environment.

Hypothetical situation
If you were to get this job, how would you fix this problem?
Comparable experience
What have you done that’s most comparable?
Exploring an experience
What was the pace like?
What was the intensity like?
Did you have enough resources?
Exploring a process
Walk me through how you approached the problem.
Exploring a decision
What was the biggest decision you made, and how was it made?
Exploring someone's contribution
What role did that person play in your success?
Exploring management style
What was your hiring manager like?
What kind of people do you like to work for?

6. Comparable ≠ Identical

Key idea:
Experience doesn't have to be identical. It just has to be comparable.

8. Creative Cascade — Choose Your Next Step

Now the chain should start building itself. Answer the question. Then choose where to take your idea next.

QUESTION
↓
ANSWER
↓
REASON
↓
EXAMPLE
↓
ADDITIONAL DETAIL
↓
CONSEQUENCE
Step 1 — Answer
Give your first answer to the question.
Step 2 — Choose
A: Why is this important?
B: Can you give an example?
Step 3 — Choose again
A: What else is important?
B: What happens if this is missing?
Step 4 — Choose again
A: Can you give a specific example?
B: Is this always true?

There is no single correct route. The goal is to keep the idea moving.

12. Final Speaking Task 🎤

What makes someone successful at work?

Speak for 1–2 minutes.

Don't plan every sentence. Let the next idea come from the previous one.

Try to use:

  • at least 5 vocabulary items;
  • at least 4 useful chunks;
  • one example;
  • one additional detail;
  • one contrast or qualification;
  • one consequence.
Vocabulary bank:

track record · comparable experience · technical competency · managerial fit · pace · intensity · resources · decision-making · accomplishment · excel · handle
Useful chunks:

I would say... · The point is... · The reason is... · For example... · Another thing is... · At the same time... · That said... · What matters is... · That's why...
