4. Interview Question Patterns

These questions help explore experience, decision-making and work environment.

Hypothetical situation
If you were to get this job, how would you fix this problem?
Comparable experience
What have you done that’s most comparable?
Exploring an experience
What was the pace like?
What was the intensity like?
Did you have enough resources?
Exploring a process
Walk me through how you approached the problem.
Exploring a decision
What was the biggest decision you made, and how was it made?
Exploring someone's contribution
What role did that person play in your success?
Exploring management style
What was your hiring manager like?
What kind of people do you like to work for?
