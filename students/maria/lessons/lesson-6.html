Lesson 06 📝

Topic: My Daily Routine

Date: September 1, 2026

🎯 Today's goals

  • Talk about your daily routine
  • Use Present Simple correctly
  • Learn useful daily routine vocabulary

1. Responsibilities & Business Impact

🧠 Vocabulary

  • business impact — влияние на бизнес
  • impact the business — влиять на бизнес
  • the business scales — бизнес масштабируется

❓ Questions

  1. What are your key responsibilities?
  2. How does your work impact the business?
  3. How do you measure your impact?
  4. What results are you responsible for?

💬 Useful frames

I’m responsible for...

My work has a direct impact on...

I like to see how my work impacts the business.

I want to understand how my work fits into the bigger picture.

📖 Grammar

We practised the Present Simple.

Example: I usually get up at 7.

Question: What time do you usually get up?

🗣 Speaking

We talked about our daily routines.

Try to answer:

  1. What time do you usually wake up?
  2. What do you do in the morning?
  3. What time do you start work?
  4. What do you usually do in the evening?
