Lesson 06 📝

Topic: Cascade Speech & Recruitment
Lesson goal:
Learn to develop an answer instead of stopping after the first sentence. Build a natural chain of ideas: answer → reason → example → additional detail → consequence.

1. Vocabulary: Simple idea → professional vocabulary

🧠 Find the professional expression

Match the simpler description with the vocabulary from the lesson. Try to remember the word before looking at the vocabulary bank.

  1. a history of good results and achievements
  2. the technical skills and knowledge needed to do a particular job
  3. how fast things happen at work
  4. the people, time, money or support available to do something
  5. the ability to make decisions
  6. experience that is similar enough to be useful in a particular situation

  10. to deal successfully with a difficult situation or problem

Choose three words from the vocabulary bank. Make a sentence about work, your experience or a hypothetical situation. Then add one more sentence.

A strong track record can be important when hiring someone.

3. Chunks: What idea does the expression help you express?

These expressions help you move from one idea to the next. Think about the function of each chunk.

Idea Useful chunks
Express an opinion I would say that... The way I see it... To me...
Explain the main idea The point is... The idea is... The reason is...
Clarify What I mean by that is... In other words...
Add another aspect Another thing is... There’s also... One thing worth mentioning is...

Show a consequence That’s why... As a result... This means that...

8. Creative Cascade — Choose Your Next Step

Now the chain should start building itself. Answer the question. Then choose where to take your idea next.

QUESTION
↓
ANSWER
↓
REASON
↓
EXAMPLE
↓
ADDITIONAL DETAIL
↓
CONSEQUENCE
Step 1 — Answer
Give your first answer to the question.
Step 2 — Choose
A: Why is this important?
B: Can you give an example?
Step 3 — Choose again
A: What else is important?
B: What happens if this is missing?
Step 4 — Choose again
A: Can you give a specific example?
B: Is this always true?

There is no single correct route. The goal is to keep the idea moving.

12. Final Speaking Task 🎤

What makes someone successful at work?

Speak for 1–2 minutes.

Don't plan every sentence. Let the next idea come from the previous one.

Try to use:

  • at least 5 vocabulary items;
  • at least 4 useful chunks;
  • one example;
  • one additional detail;
  • one contrast or qualification;
  • one consequence.
