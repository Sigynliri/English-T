🗣️ 5. Free Practice: Speaking & Discussion

💬 Debate

Lou says that a manager who is "dominant and in your face and direct" could be a problem, and so could a "micromanager". Do you agree? What management style helps you do your best work?

From the script: Lou says most managers are "coaches, delegators". Is that true in your experience?

🧠 Role-play

Work in pairs. One person is Lou, the other is a hiring manager. Lou asks: "Tell me about the best people you work for. What kind of people do you like to work for?" Then swap roles.

After the role-play, discuss: what did you learn about the other person's preferred management style?
