Daily Routine 🧠

Useful words and phrases from our lessons.

📋 ПОЛНЫЙ СПИСОК ВОКАБУЛЯРА

Выражение Перевод Пример использования
align with соответствовать Our new goals align with the company's long-term strategy.
apply for a position подать заявку на вакансию I decided to apply for a position as a senior project manager.
as for the next step что касается следующего шага As for the next step, we need to schedule a follow-up interview.
can handle different positions regardless of the industry может справляться с разными должностями независимо от отрасли An experienced manager can handle different positions regardless of the industry.
fill a position закрыть вакансию It took us three weeks to fill a position in the IT department.
fill in for someone временно заменять кого-то Could you please fill in for me at tomorrow's team meeting?
fit into the bigger picture вписываться в общую стратегию We need to see how these daily tasks fit into the bigger picture.
headquarters головной офис Our headquarters are located in New York, but we work remotely.
I just feel я просто считаю / чувствую I just feel that we need more data before making a decision.
I think we should я думаю, нам следует I think we should focus on improving the quality of our product.
I'm glad to hear it я рад это слышать You passed the test? I'm glad to hear it!
key responsibilities ключевые обязанности One of my key responsibilities is leading the development team.
likely be вероятно, буду I will likely be responsible for the final project results.
open position открытая вакансия We currently have an open position for a QA engineer.
parent company материнская компания Our parent company provides financial support for this startup.
provide additional value создавать дополнительную ценность New features will provide additional value to our loyal customers.
provide real value приносить реальную пользу We want to build a product that provides real value to users.
provide value to customers приносить пользу клиентам Our main goal is always to provide value to customers.
provide value to users приносить пользу пользователям The app update was designed to provide value to users instantly.
related to doc/website/ownership: the doc of mine связано с документами/сайтом/собственностью: мой документ Please review this project proposal, it's a doc of mine.
room (to negotiate) пространство для манёвра The budget is tight, so we don't have much room to negotiate.
Someone would argue that некоторые могут возразить Someone would argue that remote work is less effective than office work.
So as far as next step итак, что касается следующего шага So as far as next step, we are going to test the new code.
step up and take responsibility взять на себя ответственность It's time to step up and take responsibility for these project results.
step up to the challenge принять вызов The team managed to step up to the challenge and meet the deadline.
step up to the position / the role занять должность / взять на себя роль She will step up to the role of team lead next week.
step up when needed проявить инициативу, когда нужно He is a great team player who always steps up when needed.
Sustain (maintain / preserve or save in good quality) поддерживать / сохранять в хорошем качестве It is hard to sustain high quality without constant regular testing.
sustain growth поддерживать рост The company is trying to sustain growth in a competitive market.
sustain interest поддерживать интерес We need a strong marketing campaign to sustain interest in our app.
sustain momentum поддерживать темп Let's double our efforts to sustain momentum until the project is finished.
sustain motivation поддерживать мотивацию Good bonuses help employees sustain motivation during difficult periods.
sustain performance поддерживать производительность Proper rest helps our team sustain high performance over time.
sustain progress сохранять прогресс We must keep working hard to sustain progress made last month.
The main reason is основная причина в том, что The main reason is that we need to sustain the project's results.
We'd like to offer мы хотели бы предложить We'd like to offer you a flexible hybrid work schedule.
will be found будут указаны / найдены All the necessary instructions will be found in the attached document.
