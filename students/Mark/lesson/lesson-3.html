Meet the Sound of A!

Hi, Mark! Let's listen, look and discover!

1 · Closed syllable

A says /æ/

A syllable can end with a consonant. In many short words, A makes the sound /æ/.

🐱
cat /kæt/
🐶
dog /dɒɡ/

Listen! Which word has the /æ/ sound?

Teacher's note: Say “cat” and “dog” aloud. Ask Mark to listen, then choose. He does not need to read the words himself.
2 · Open syllable

A says its name: /eɪ/

Look at ba-by. The first syllable ends with A. A often says its name in an open syllable.

👶
ba-by First syllable: /beɪ/
🎂
cake A = /eɪ/

Which word has the same vowel sound as baby?

Teacher's note: Say “baby”, “cake” and “map”. Ask Mark to find the word with the same vowel sound as the first syllable of “baby”. Explain that cake uses the a_e pattern, not an open syllable.
3 · Magic E

What happens when we add E?

Compare cap and cape. The final E is usually silent, but it changes the sound of A.

🧢
cap A = /æ/
🦸
cape A = /eɪ/

What happens when we add E?

Teacher's note: Say “cap” and “cape”. Point to A in each word, then reveal the final E. Let Mark hear the difference before explaining the pattern.
